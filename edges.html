Sorted by kickoff. Spread and O/U are the current line (ESPN when available, otherwise the weekly cfbfastR consensus); implied team totals come from them, favorite = (O/U + spread) ÷ 2. Line move compares the weekly consensus line at this week's first update with the latest update. Net EPA = offense EPA/play minus defense EPA/play allowed (from cfbfastR play-by-play); the column names the team with the better net number and the gap between the two. Plays / G adds both teams' plays per game; the colored figure is the difference from the FBS average (blue = faster game). Early pass % is each team's share of 1st- and 2nd-down plays that were passes. Non-FBS teams have no stats, so their games show dashes there.
